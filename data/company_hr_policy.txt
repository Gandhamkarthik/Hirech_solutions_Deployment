# Company HR Policy

============================
Section 1: Introduction & Core Values
============================
Our organization is built on the pillars of innovation, integrity, and inclusivity. We believe that a diverse workforce brings a multitude of perspectives that drive our success. All employees are expected to uphold these values in every interaction, whether with colleagues, clients, or partners. We strive to create an environment where everyone feels safe to express their ideas and take calculated risks to move the company forward.

============================
Section 2: Recruitment and Hiring
============================
The hiring process at our company is designed to be meritocratic and transparent. All open positions are posted internally first to encourage growth within the organization. Candidates undergo a series of technical assessments and behavioral interviews to ensure a strong fit for both the role and the company culture. We are committed to equal opportunity employment and actively seek to eliminate bias from our hiring pipelines through blinded resume reviews and structured interviewing panels.

============================
Section 3: Working Hours and Attendance
============================
Our standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. However, we recognize that flexibility is key to productivity and well-being. Employees may request a flexible start/end time (e.g., 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM) with approval from their direct supervisor. Punctuality is valued, but we prioritize output over hours spent at a desk. Core hours from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM are designated for team meetings and collaborative work to ensure maximum availability.

============================
Section 4: Leave Policy and Time Off
============================
Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly. Sick leave is provided separately, with 10 days allocated annually for health-related absences. We also offer a "Wellness Day" on the first Friday of every quarter, where the office closes early to encourage mental health breaks. Requests for extended leave (more than 5 consecutive days) should be submitted via the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for planning purposes.

============================
Section 5: Performance Management and Growth
============================
Performance is reviewed bi-annually through a 360-degree feedback system. This includes self-evaluations, manager reviews, and peer feedback. We avoid rigid rankings and instead focus on growth trajectories and specific KPIs. Every employee is encouraged to create a Personal Development Plan (PDP) annually, which may include budget for certifications, workshops, or advanced degrees. Promotions are based on a combination of sustained high performance and the demonstration of leadership qualities.
